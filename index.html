Privacy Policy

Effective date: July 3, 2026

Cubean (“the app”) is a mobile puzzle game developed by Yusuf Hazerhan Çömez. This policy explains what the app does and does not collect.

Data We Collect

  • Advertising identifiers. The app shows ads via Google AdMob. AdMob may use device identifiers to serve and measure ads. We serve non-personalized ads by default. See Google’s policy: policies.google.com/privacy
  • Purchase history. In-app purchases are processed entirely by Apple. We do not receive or store your payment details.
  • Game Center. If you sign in to Game Center, your scores are submitted to Apple leaderboards under Apple’s privacy terms.
  • Game progress. Your level progress and settings are stored on your device and in your personal iCloud account. We do not have access to them.

What We Do NOT Do

  • We do not collect names, emails, contacts, photos, or location.
  • We do not sell data.
  • We do not track you across other companies’ apps or websites.

Children

  • The app is rated 4+ and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.
